    Why Mason

    Move it once,
    to where it’s needed.

    On a construction site, materials can be unloaded, stored, staged and then delivered before anyone uses them. Every extra move costs time and labour.

    01

    Fewer moves

    Mason carries materials from storage straight to the work area, so there are fewer stops in between.

    02

    The hauls other equipment misses

    Forklifts, telehandlers and carts suit some jobs. Mason is for the short, repeated hauls where they’re a poor fit.

    03

    There when the crew is ready

    Materials staged early often get moved again. Mason brings them to the point of use when the crew needs them.
